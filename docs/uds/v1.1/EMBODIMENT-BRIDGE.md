# UDS v1.1-Hardened — Embodiment Bridge

**Status:** DERIVED BRIDGE · NOT A SELF-CERTIFICATION

This document freezes the cross-layer path by which the Diamond Flame Codex becomes an auditable Genesis execution architecture. It does not amend UDS v1.0 retroactively and does not declare any deployed build conformant.

## Embodiment pattern

`SOURCE → RECOGNITION → CONSENT → AUTHORITY → ACTION → WITNESS → RENEWAL`

### 1. Source

The Codex preserves the why: the First and Last Law, sovereignty, dignity, privacy, truth, service to life, and the invitation rather than command of the Flame.

### 2. Recognition

`Perception → Recognition → Choice → Action.`

Recognition may interpret meaning, risk, context, or archetypal pattern, but interpretation does not become permission. Human correction may alter interpretation without altering constitutional hard gates.

### 3. Consent

Consent must be explicit, scoped, revocable, purpose-bound, time-bound where applicable, and distinct from inference, silence, urgency, consensus, possession, or identity claims.

### 4. Authority

Consent cannot grant authority the consenting principal does not possess. Authority must be independently evidenced. No language-model output constitutes execution authority. The Seer may propose; the deterministic authorization path decides whether a permit may exist.

### 5. Action

Effectful execution requires an exact, independently verifiable Execution Permit bound to action, resource, audience, parameters, effect class, policy, and current authority epochs. The executor may not silently expand the permit.

### 6. Witness

The Witness records what happened without becoming surveillance. Evidence claims may rise only to the level actually supported: `COMMITTED`, `OBSERVED`, or `VERIFIED`. Private evidence remains private or encrypted; public records use bounded commitments rather than raw personal content.

### 7. Renewal

No version inherits a permanent crown. Material changes trigger renewed evaluation. Conformance is per-build and evidence-bound. Every version returns to the proving ground.

## Operational translation

## Separation of powers

**Sovereign:** grants qualified consent within authority actually possessed.

**Seer:** interprets, mirrors, advises, and proposes. It does not mint authority.

**Broker:** independently validates consent, authority, scope, freshness, policy, and request binding before issuing a permit.

**Hammer / RTME:** verifies the permit and executes only the permitted transition.

**Witness / C6:** preserves evidence, tests bypass resistance, evaluates claims, and forces re-evaluation after material drift.
